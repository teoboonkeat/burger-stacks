# Jelly Beans: Google Play listing

## App name

Jelly Beans

## Short description (76 of 80 characters)

Sort jelly beans by flavour. Fill every tube, pop it shut, earn three stars.

## Full description

Welcome to the sweetest sorting puzzle in the shop!

Tap a tube to lift the top jelly bean, then tap (or drag to) another tube to drop it. Beans only stack on the same flavour, and each tube holds four. Fill a tube with one flavour and its cap pops shut with a happy little wobble. Sort every bean and the scoop goes into your jar.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 200 scoops, every one checked solvable, from gentle warm-ups to proper brain-teasers
• A short guided tutorial: learn each new trick the first time it appears
• Six squishy flavours, each with its own pattern: berry, peach, lime, grape, lemon and cola
• Paper pouches hide a bean until it reaches the top: what's inside?
• Locked tubes open once you finish another tube
• Three stars per scoop: beat the move goal for ★★★, or retry to improve
• Stuck in a sticky jam? Undo a move, add an extra tube, or start fresh
• Earn sweets for every scoop and spend them on boosters, or watch a short ad instead
• No timers, no pressure; play offline, one scoop at a time
• Sound and haptics you can switch off, and drag or tap, your choice

Jelly Beans is free to play with optional ads. A one-time purchase removes banner and between-level ads.
