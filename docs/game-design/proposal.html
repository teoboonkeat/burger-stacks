Game design proposal · Jelly Beans @ 65ed62f (round 2 @ 8489160) · 200 scoops

Something to fill, something to finish

"Players don't feel they progress, or don't have a larger goal to work towards."

Diagnosis from the code, the level data, 8,000 simulated playthroughs (40 per scoop) and the running web build at four save states (scoop 2, 15, 60, 150). Nothing in the project was changed. Proposals are labelled P1 to P5; answer with the ids. Round 2 (added later, at the end of the page) adds P6 to P13, a combination table and a recommended bundle.

Diagnosis

The game stops giving news at scoop 21

  • Nothing new for 179 scoops. The last mechanic (locked tube) arrives at scoop 21. From there to 200 the player gets the same screen, the same rewards and no milestone. The step up in difficulty at 101 (more pouches, a lock on every board) comes without a marker or a reward.
  • Home has no goal. It shows the jar, a star count, a sweets count and "Play · Scoop 60". Nothing says what the next finish line is.
  • Stars are decoration. 600 stars exist; the count is shown on home and the level list, but gates, unlocks and rewards ignore it. "Retry for ★★★" is worth 5 to 10 sweets.
  • Sweets have nothing desirable to buy. The only sinks are three boosters (20 to 40), each also free for an ad, plus a free undo every scoop. Balances pile up (about 1,000 by scoop 60) and the number stops meaning anything.
  • The level list is one flat grid of 200 tiles. No chapters, no sections, nothing to complete. Rush scoops (every 10th from 40 to 100) look like any other tile.
  • Replays pay full price. Scoop 2 (par 3, about 10 seconds) pays 25 sweets every time, 50 with the ad. Harmless today because sweets buy little; it breaks any sweets sink we add.

Proposals, ranked

Five ways to give the long game a shape

Genre patterns borrowed: chapters with a finish line, a meter that fills from stars, a cosmetic shop as the currency sink, a daily goal with a streak. Names, art and numbers below are this game's own.

P1

Bean Jars: 20 chapters of 10 scoops

Recommended firstEffort M

Every 10 scoops fill one jar. Each finished scoop pours a handful of beans into the current jar, on the result card and on home. The 10th scoop is the Lid scoop (today's rush scoops at 40 to 100; a normal scoop elsewhere) with its own tile and HUD label. Winning it seals the jar, plays a short celebration and puts the jar on the Pantry shelf, a row of 20 jar slots on home that fills up over the game.

Solves
The 179-scoop plateau: a finish line every 10 scoops, a long goal (20 jars) visible on home, unmarked rush scoops.
Numbers
  • Seal a jar: +50 sweets + 1 free Extra tube (stock, used before paying).
  • Every 5th jar (50, 100, 150, 200): +150 sweets and a gold lid on the shelf.
  • Lid scoop win: +10 on top of the normal pay.
  • Adds about 9 sweets per scoop on average (1,800 over 200 scoops).
Variants
P1a jars, shelf and seal reward (above). P1b = P1a plus a jar-coloured header and chapter sections in the level list, with "8/10 · 21/30★" per jar. P1b adds about a day.
Existing saves
Jars below currentLevel count as sealed with no back-pay (or one catch-up gift, open question 3).
Files
new lib/app/chapters.dart (pure rules + test); app_state.dart (new keys jarsSealed, boosterStock); screens.dart (home jar card, shelf, list sections); game_screen.dart (result jar progress, seal overlay, Lid label); widgets.dart (Jar with fill level); shop.dart (spend free stock first).
Risks
Seal overlay adds one more screen after a win (keep it under 2 s, tap to skip). Free Extra tubes lower ad views slightly (about 20 over the game).

P2

Star treat box: stars fill a meter

Effort S

A star meter sits under the jar card on home and on the result card. Each new star (first clears and improved replays) adds to it. At 30 stars the treat box opens with a short shake and pop, then the meter starts again.

Solves
Stars mean nothing; "Retry for ★★★" has no pull.
Numbers
  • Box every 30 stars: 40 sweets, alternating with 1 free Peek or Undo×3.
  • Casual player (1.5★): a box every 20 scoops. Careful player (2.5★): every 12. 20 boxes in the whole game.
  • Adds about 2 to 3 sweets per scoop.
Existing saves
The meter starts at starTotal mod 30; past boxes are not paid out.
Files
new lib/app/rewards.dart + test; app_state.dart (key starBoxes); screens.dart (home meter); game_screen.dart (result meter and box pop).
Risks
Low: stars only count once per scoop, so replays cannot farm boxes. Adds one more meter to home; keep it thin.

P3

Effort M

A Shop button on home. Sweets buy small cosmetic swaps drawn in code: tube cap colours, backdrop dot patterns and jar lids for the shelf. All are variants of the existing palette, so the game keeps its look. Owned items show a green edge, the equipped one a raspberry ring.

Solves
Sweets have no desirable sink; 1,000+ idle sweets by scoop 60.
Numbers
  • 12 items: 4 tube caps at 150, 4 backdrops at 400, 4 jar lids at 800. Total 5,400.
  • With P1+P2 a casual player earns about 5,600 over 200 scoops (no ads), so the shop lasts the whole game. First cap is affordable around scoop 7, first backdrop around 22.
Files

Effort S to M

A small card on home: "Today: win 3 scoops". Done gives sweets and ticks the day. Seven ticked days in a row give a bigger treat. Missing a day restarts the week; no penalty beyond that.

Solves
A reason to come back tomorrow (mid loop); today there is none.
Numbers

P5

Replays pay only for better stars

Effort SPairs with P1

The first win pays as today. A replay pays the difference between the new result and the best so far: 1★ to 3★ pays +10, 2★ to 3★ pays +5, no improvement pays 0. The result card says so: "Best was ★★ · +5 for the new star".

Solves
Farming (about 150 sweets a minute on scoop 2). Required before P3, so a 150-sweet item cannot be bought in a minute.
Variant
P5b: flat +5 per replay with no improvement, so replays never feel worthless. Caps farming at about 30 sweets a minute.
Files
app_state.dart (completeLevel returns the pay); game_screen.dart (_won, result copy); test for the pay rule.
Risks
Players used to replay income notice the drop; the ×2 ad should double the actual pay only.

Recommended first step

Build P1a with P5

Jars give the most visible change for the work: a progress bar that moves after every win, a finish line every 10 scoops, a 20-jar shelf as the long goal, and the rush scoops finally marked. P5 is a few lines and stops farming before any sink arrives. Then P2 (stars matter), then P3 once sweets have a reason to be saved. P4 last, and only if return rate is the next complaint.

Open questions
  1. Replays: P5 (pay only the star difference), P5b (flat +5) or keep full pay?
  2. Is a free booster stock (Extra tube from jars, Peek from treat boxes) acceptable, or should rewards be sweets only?
  3. Existing players: mark past jars sealed silently, or give one catch-up gift (for example 50 sweets per past jar, capped at 300)?
  4. Jar size 10 (20 jars) or 20 (10 jars, longer chapters, bigger rewards)?
  5. Should Lid scoops on 101 to 200 also get the rush layout (harder), or stay ordinary with only the label?
  6. The difficulty step at 101 has nothing new for the player. Is a new board mechanic for 101+ in scope later (level design work)?
  7. For P3, which cosmetics are allowed: tube caps, backdrops, jar lids? Bean skins are out, since the bean colours carry the puzzle.
Round 2 · added after the owner asked for more

Eight more ways: P6 to P13

P1 to P5 above give the game a shelf, a meter, a shop, a daily habit and a fair replay rule. They are all rewards around the puzzle. The options below go further: new things to play (P6), a place to see the whole journey (P7), an identity that grows (P8), things to collect (P9), a skill goal for replays (P10), events (P11), a reason to play that is about someone else (P12) and a way back for lapsed players (P13). None repeats P1 to P5; where one overlaps, its card says so.

P6

A new kitchen twist every 20 scoops

Recommended in this roundP6a Effort MP6b Effort L

Every 20 scoops after 21, a new twist arrives. It gets a one-time lesson card (same coach as today's lessons), a "New: …" banner on its first board, and then it is mixed into the boards that follow. Each twist starts with 2 easier scoops before it shows up on hard boards. The step at 101 becomes a named event, the Big Kitchen, instead of a silent wall.

Solves
The root cause: 179 scoops with nothing new and an unexplained difficulty cliff at 101.
Schedule
  • 41 Double lock: the padlock shows "2"; open it by sealing 2 tubes. Data only (unlockAt: 2); the padlock already draws the number (jelly_game.dart:871).
  • 61 Tall tubes: 5 beans per tube on some boards. Data only (capacity), plus a check that the beans stay readable at 360 px.
  • 101 Big Kitchen: 6 flavours and 8 tubes, as today, but marked with a banner and a lesson, and 101 to 103 are breathers.
  • 141 Double lock + tall tubes on the same board.
  • P6b adds new rules: 81 Label tube (a "Lime only" sticker, so only lime may enter); 121 Sticky beans (a glossy bean moves on its own, never as part of a group); 161 mixes of the two.
Variants
P6a: double lock, tall tubes, Big Kitchen. Uses only rules that exist today, so the core rules stay as they are. P6b: P6a plus the label tube and sticky beans. These are new board rules, so the owner has to approve them as a change to the core rules.
Save keys
None new. The lesson names (lock2, tall, bigKitchen, later label and sticky) go into the existing tutorialSeen list. A player who is already past 41 has a saved list, so they get each lesson the first time they meet the twist, which is what we want.
Files
assets/levels/levels.json (re-tune 41 to 200); lib/levels/generator.dart (tierFor: twist schedule, and port the hand-tuning first, per the HANDOFF caveat); lib/app/tutorial.dart (new Lesson values and new LevelFacts fields); lib/ui/coach.dart (lesson cards); lib/ui/game_screen.dart (New banner); test/levels_test.dart (par is still optimal, twist counts per range). P6b also changes lib/logic/board.dart (movable, groupSize), lib/logic/solver.dart (_key must include labels, because it sorts tubes), lib/levels/level.dart (JSON fields), lib/game/jelly_game.dart and bean_painter.dart (sticker and gloss).
Risks
This is the largest content change. Re-tuning scoops a player has already 3-starred changes their boards, but the stars they earned stay. Every new board needs the difficulty simulation run again, so the cliff at 101 is not rebuilt. Tall tubes on 360x640 need a layout check. Sticky beans (P6b) could feel arbitrary unless the gloss is clear.
With P1–P5
Very good with P1: twists land on jar boundaries (jar 5, 7, 11 …) and the jar card can preview "Next jar: Tall tubes". The P2/P3/P4/P5 economies are unaffected.

P7

Candy Road: the level list as a journey

Effort M

The Levels screen becomes a winding road drawn in code, running bottom to top. Each scoop is a stop on the road, and every 10th stop is a jar landmark. Signposts mark what is coming ("Tall tubes ahead · 61"). The player's marker walks one stop forward after each new win, the first time the screen opens after that win. The star count on the app bar stays.

Solves
The flat 200-tile grid. Players see how far they have come, the finish at 200, and what the next twist or landmark is.
Numbers
  • 20 sections of 10 stops; a signpost 10 stops before each P6 twist; rush and Lid scoops drawn as bigger stops.
  • No rewards of its own. It only shows the rewards of the other options.
Save keys
roadSeen (int): the last stop the marker was drawn at, so the walking animation plays once. Default is currentLevel, so existing players see no animation.
Files
new lib/ui/road_screen.dart (a CustomPainter path plus a lazy list of stops); lib/ui/screens.dart (Levels opens the road; LevelGrid.offsetFor becomes the road's scroll maths); lib/app/app_state.dart; a test for the stop positions and the scroll offset.
Risks
200 stops need lazy drawing to keep frames under 16 ms. Tablet and landscape need a wider road. A grid jumps faster to a given scoop; keep a "Jump to scoop" chip.
With P1–P5
It replaces P1b's list sections (choose one; P7 is the bigger change). It is best with P1a (jars as landmarks) and P6 (signposts).

P8

Confectioner rank: a level for the player

Effort SAlternative to P2

A rank badge with a thin XP bar on home and on the result card: "Rank 11 · Jelly Apprentice". Everything that counts as progress adds XP. Each rank-up shows a short card with the new title and a small gift. There are 25 ranks with titles from "Bean Sorter" to "Grand Confectioner".

Solves
"Am I getting better?" One number that rises after every win. Stars get a use without a separate box meter.
Numbers
  • XP: 10 per first clear, 5 per new star, 30 per sealed jar (P1), 10 per Master spoon (P10). Replays give XP only for new stars, so XP cannot be farmed.
  • Rank n needs 15·n(n+1)/2 XP (rank 10 = 825, rank 20 = 3,150, rank 25 = 4,875).
  • Casual player (1.5★ per scoop, with P1): rank 4 by scoop 10, 11 by 50, 16 by 100, 22 by 200. A rank-up every 2 to 4 scoops early, every 10 around scoop 50 and every 15 late. Careful player (2.5★): rank 25 at about scoop 190.
  • Rank-up gift: +25 sweets, about 550 over the game.
Save keys
xpBonus (int: XP not derivable from stars and clears, such as jars and spoons) and rankPaid (int: highest rank already paid). XP from stars and clears is worked out from stars, so existing players get their rank at once. rankPaid starts at that rank, so there is no back-pay (or a catch-up gift; see question R2-3).
Files
new lib/app/rank.dart (pure: XP, thresholds, titles) + test; app_state.dart; screens.dart (home badge); game_screen.dart (SweetScreen XP bar, rank-up card); widgets.dart (badge).
Risks
One more meter. If P2 is also built, home shows two star-driven bars. So either choose P8 instead of P2, or move P2's box into every 3rd rank-up.
With P1–P5
It overlaps P2 (choose one). It feeds from P1 and P10, and P5 keeps it fair.

P9

Bean Album: sticker pages to complete

Effort MSweets sink

An album of 48 named bean stickers on 8 pages of 6, such as "Lime Fizz", "Cola Float" and "Peach Cobbler". Each sticker is drawn in code: one of the 6 bean colours plus a pattern (stripes, speckles, swirl), so the puzzle colours are never changed. Stickers come in packs of 3. A full page pays a prize and puts a gold edge on it.

Solves
No collection and nothing worth buying. It is a sweets sink that grows with progress, because new pages only open as you advance.
Numbers
  • Page k opens at scoop 25(k−1)+1 (pages at 1, 26, 51 … 176). Packs only hold stickers from open pages.
  • Free packs: 1 per sealed jar (P1), 1 every 5th first-clear ★★★. A pack costs 120 sweets in the shop.
  • Duplicates refund 10 sweets. Fairness rule: once a page has 4 of its 6 stickers, packs give only stickers you do not have.
  • A full page pays +100 sweets and a jar-lid colour for the shelf (it feeds P3). Estimate: free packs fill about 60% of the album over 200 scoops, so the rest takes about 8 to 10 bought packs (1,000 to 1,200 sweets).
Save keys
album (string list of sticker ids) and packs (int, unopened). Existing players get 1 free pack per page already open.
Files
new lib/app/album.dart (pure: pages, drop rule, fair-pick, seeded Random) + test; lib/game/bean_painter.dart (patterned sticker bean, outside the board only); new lib/ui/album_screen.dart; screens.dart (Album button with a "1 pack" badge); game_screen.dart (pack on the result card); shop.dart (pack price in Prices).
Risks
Random packs can feel like gambling. Keep them sweets-only (never IAP), show "new until 4/6", and show the odds. It needs P5 or P5b, or replays buy the whole album. Writing 48 names and patterns is content work.
With P1–P5
It fits P1 (jar = pack) and P3 (page prize = lid). It competes with P3 for sweets (both together are fine if prices stay as here). It needs P5/P5b.

P10

Master spoons: ★★★ with no help

Effort SPairs with P5

A scoop you have already cleared shows a spoon outline on its tile. Win it at par without Undo, Peek or Extra tube and the spoon turns gold. Each jar shows "Spoons 4/10". Today Undo gives the move back, so ★★★ can be reached by trial and error. The spoon is the clean version, a skill goal for players who have already 3-starred a scoop.

Solves
Replays have no goal once P5 cuts their pay. It gives skilled players a long target (200 spoons) without new content.
Numbers
  • First gold spoon on a scoop: +10 sweets (+10 XP with P8). At most 2,000 sweets, but in practice a few hundred.
  • All 10 spoons in a jar: the jar on the shelf gets a gold rim (P1) and pays +50.
  • The spoon opens only after the first clear, so on a replay the player already knows what the pouches hide. That matters because par assumes the solver knows the pouches (solver.dart).
Save keys
spoons (string list of scoop ids). Starts empty; there is no way to award it retroactively, since past runs did not record booster use.
Files
game_screen.dart (add undoUsed next to the existing peekUsed, extraUsed at line 40; spoon in _won and SweetScreen); app_state.dart; screens.dart (_LevelTile spoon); a small rule test.
Risks
Small. Late boards at par with no undo are hard (an 18 to 26% sim win rate on 181 to 200 even with any number of moves), so expect few late spoons. That is fine for an optional goal, but the free undo must be counted as help, and the tile must say so.
With P1–P5
It gives P5's zero-pay replays a reason, and a jar goal on top of P1. It feeds P8. It does not conflict with P2/P3/P4.

P11

Weekly Bake-off: 5 fixed boards a week

Effort M to LAlternative to P4

A home card opens a set of 5 special boards that changes every Monday. They are the same for every player, get harder from 1 to 5, and each one uses this week's theme twist (for example "Double locks week"). Winning all 5 puts a weekly ribbon on the shelf. The boards do not touch the main 200.

Solves
No events or milestones. It is a reason to come back each week, and content for players who have finished 200.
Numbers
  • Each board: +20 sweets on the first win (P5 rule on replays). All 5: +150 and a ribbon. At most 250 a week.
  • 52 weeks × 5 = 260 boards, made offline with the generator and the ported tuning rules, checked by the par test. A week is chosen from the local date (ISO week number modulo 52).
Save keys
weekId (int) and weekDone (string list of board indexes), reset when weekId changes; ribbons (int).
Files
new assets/levels/weekly.json (about 165 KB; the 200 main boards are 128 KB); new lib/app/weekly.dart (pure, clock injected) + test; tool/generate_levels.dart (weekly output); main.dart (load it); game_screen.dart (play a board outside the main list: today level is an index into s.levels, so this needs a level source); screens.dart (home card).
Risks
GameScreen assumes main-list levels (it does level++ after a win). It inherits the generator caveat in HANDOFF.md. Calendar exploits (changing the device clock) are accepted, as in P4. The boards repeat after a year.
With P1–P5
It overlaps P4: both are calendar habits, so build one first. It fits P6 (weekly themes reuse the twists) and P5.

P12

Sweet orders: fill a customer's jar

Effort M

A small Orders card on home holds up to 3 order slips from named regulars, for example "Gran's tea party: 6 lemon, 4 berry". Every tube you seal in any win counts toward a matching slip, replays included. A filled slip pays sweets and adds one line to that customer's short story ("Gran: The grandkids loved them!"). Orders are words only, with no new character art, so the look stays as it is.

Solves
Gives a reason to play that is about someone else, and gives replays a purpose under P5. The goals change all the time, so there is always something nearly done.
Numbers
  • One new slip every 5 scoops of progress (max 3 open), so a player cannot get slips faster than they progress, even by replaying. 40 slips over the game.
  • A slip asks for 6 to 12 tubes of 1 or 2 flavours (there are about 5.5 sealed tubes per board; the flavours are equal, 180 to 187 tubes each in levels.json). Pay is 4 sweets per tube (24 to 48).
  • 8 regulars × 5 story lines. Finishing a customer's 5th slip earns a thank-you card in the album (P9) or a shelf item.
  • About 1,400 sweets over the game.
Save keys

P13

Comeback hooks: welcome-back gift and gentle reminders

P13a Effort SP13b Effort M

P13a: after 2 or more days away, home opens with a "Welcome back" card. It gives a gift and one line about the nearest goal ("Jar 6 is 3 scoops from sealed"). P13b: optional local reminders, at most 1 a day and never at night, that point at a real goal: the daily treat (P4), a jar that is almost sealed (P1), or the week ending (P11). They are off until the player turns them on from that card.

Solves
Lapsed players have nothing pulling them back. Today, after a break, the player gets a cold "Play · Scoop 60".
Numbers
  • Gift: +30 sweets after 2 to 6 days away, +60 and 1 free Extra tube after 7 or more. It can be claimed at most once every 7 days, so it pays no more than an active player earns.
  • Reminders: 1 a day at most, the player's usual play hour, and they stop after 3 that are ignored.
Save keys
lastPlayDay (int, the local date as yyyymmdd; it starts at today, so nobody gets a gift on the first launch after the update), welcomeClaimedDay (int), and remindersOn (bool, false) for P13b.
Files
new lib/app/comeback.dart (pure, clock injected) + test; screens.dart (welcome card); main.dart (didChangeAppLifecycleState already saves on pause, so set the day there). Risks
P13a: very low. P13b: a new native dependency, the runtime permission dialog, Play policy on notification content, and nothing to try on the web build. Reminders with nothing behind them annoy players, so build P13b only after P1 or P4.
